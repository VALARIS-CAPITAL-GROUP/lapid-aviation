FARNBOROUGH AIRPORT

Private jet charter from Farnborough Airport

Farnborough is a dedicated business aviation airport south-west of London, used almost exclusively for private and corporate flights rather than scheduled traffic.

Why travellers use it

A dedicated business aviation terminal means no scheduled passenger traffic to queue behind, and handling built around private flights rather than accommodating them.

Business travel

The most requested departure point for corporate flights, given its proximity to west London and the M3 corridor.

Leisure travel

A straightforward, low-fuss start to a European or long-haul leisure trip, without the scale of a commercial airport.

Common questions

Is Farnborough only for business jets?

No — it's used for leisure charter too. It simply doesn't handle scheduled airline traffic, so it suits any private flight regardless of purpose.

WHY LAPID

There are three ways to charter a jet. Only one of them works for you.

We would rather explain the trade-offs than pretend they do not exist. Here is what the alternatives get right, and where they leave you.

BOOK DIRECT WITH AN OPERATOR
Their fleet, their answer

An operator will always recommend an aircraft it owns. If the right one for your sector is parked with somebody else, you will not hear about it.

AN ONLINE BOOKING PLATFORM
Fast until something moves

Instant pricing is a real advantage. Then the meeting shifts, the aircraft goes technical, or the luggage does not fit, and there is nobody who knows your trip.

LAPID AVIATION
A broker with no fleet to defend

We hold no aircraft, so we have no reason to steer you. We search the market for your specific flight, present the options with their drawbacks stated, and stay on the line when the plan changes.

You speak to the person doing the work

Not a switchboard, not a shared inbox. The broker who quoted your flight is the one who answers on the morning of departure.

Options, not a single price

You see the shortlist with what each aircraft and airport costs you in time, money and comfort, so the decision is yours on the facts.

Built around your day

The departure time, the airport, the aircraft — all chosen to fit your schedule, not the other way round.

Every operator checked first

Flights are operated by appropriately licensed third-party operators, reviewed before an option reaches you rather than after.

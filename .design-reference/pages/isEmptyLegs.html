EMPTY LEG FLIGHTS

Private jet empty leg flights

An empty leg is a flight an aircraft is already making without passengers — repositioning after a charter, or returning to base. Sold at a reduced rate, with less flexibility than booking the route from scratch.

Why they're cheaper

The aircraft is flying that route regardless. Filling the seats offsets a cost the operator would otherwise absorb, so the price reflects that, not the full charter rate.

Fixed route and timing

You take the route and departure window the aircraft is already flying. There's no negotiating the airport or the time, the way there is on a standard charter.

Can be cancelled

If the original charter that created the empty leg changes or is cancelled, the empty leg usually goes with it. It suits flexible plans, not fixed ones.

Short notice

Empty legs are usually confirmed a few days out at most, sometimes less. They suit travellers who can move on short notice.

Common questions

How much cheaper is an empty leg?

It varies by route and operator. We don't publish a fixed discount because it isn't one — each empty leg is priced on its own terms.

Can I change the date once booked?

Usually not, or only within narrow limits. The date is fixed to the original charter creating the leg.

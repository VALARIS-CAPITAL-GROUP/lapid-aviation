ABOUT LAPID AVIATION

An independent broker, not an operator

Lapid Aviation arranges private jet and helicopter charter from London through appropriately licensed third-party operators. We own no aircraft, so there is no fleet steering the advice you get — only the route, the group and the day.

What a broker does

We take a brief, search the market, check the operator, and manage the arrangement from enquiry to arrival. The aircraft is never ours — it belongs to the operator flying it.

Why that matters

An operator recommends its own aircraft. We have none to recommend, so the shortlist we send you is built around your flight, not our fleet.

Who you deal with

The broker who quotes your flight stays on it through to boarding. Company registration and regulatory details are published here once confirmed.

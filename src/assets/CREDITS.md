## Before launch — things to check

- **Verify the licence yourself.** The Pexels License is permissive, but it does
  not grant model or property releases, and it does not override trademark law.
  Do not take this file as legal advice.
- **Identifiable aircraft.** Several frames show real registrations and operator
  liveries. On a broker's site that could imply a commercial relationship that
  does not exist. Commissioned photography, or frames without legible
  registrations, avoids the question entirely.
- **Recognisable people.** `cabin-work.jpg` and `business-hero.jpg` show models.
  They read as generic business travellers rather than as Lapid clients or
  staff, which is the honest use, but commissioned imagery is better.
- **No stock people are used as the team.** The three team slots on `/about/`
  deliberately still render "photo to follow" placeholders. Presenting stock
  models as named Lapid brokers would misrepresent the company.
